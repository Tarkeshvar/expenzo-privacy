Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Expenzo is built with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, upload, or share any of your personal or financial data. Everything stays on your device.

01 Information We Collect

Expenzo does not collect any personal information. All data you enter into the app — including transactions, budgets, categories, and profile settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

We do not have access to this data at any time.

02 Data Storage

All app data is saved locally on your device. This includes:

  • Transaction history (income and expenses)
  • Budget configurations
  • Profile name and currency preferences
  • Category data

Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data. We recommend exporting important records before uninstalling.

03 Third-Party Services

Expenzo does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. No data is sent to or shared with any external party.

04 Device Permissions

Expenzo does not request access to any sensitive device features including:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Location services
  • SMS or phone

05 Internet Access

Expenzo does not require an internet connection to function. No data is transmitted over the internet at any point.

06 Children's Privacy

Expenzo does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The app contains no user accounts, no login system, and no data transmission of any kind.

07 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

08 Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us.
